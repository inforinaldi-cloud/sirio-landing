Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Cookies are small text files that websites save on the user's device for technical, statistical or profiling purposes. This page explains which cookies and similar technologies are used on this site.

1. Cookies and technologies used by this site

This site is built as a static page and does not install its own technical session cookies. It does, however, use a third-party tracking tool described in the next point, aimed at reconstructing where contacts come from (traffic-source attribution).

2. Third-party services

Some features rely on external services that, when their respective resources are loaded, may collect technical data (such as the IP address) or set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.) — loading of the typefaces. This involves a request to Google's servers that may record the IP address.
  • Case-study videos hosted on Google Cloud Storage and on the GoHighLevel platform — when a video is played, cookies may be set or technical data collected by those providers.
  • Contact form submission — the data entered in the form is transmitted to the FormSubmit service, which delivers it by email to the controller; it may also flow into the GoHighLevel (CRM) platform. These services may use technical cookies necessary for their provision.
  • GoHighLevel attribution tracking (LeadConnector / msgsndr.com) — this site loads the external-tracking.js script that sets cookies and identifiers to record page visits and the source of origin (e.g. UTM parameters, referrer), so as to associate each contact with the channel they came from. It has an analytics and marketing purpose.

3. How to manage cookies

You can manage or disable cookies directly from your browser settings. Below are the guides for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Disabling third-party cookies does not affect the browsing of the site's main content.

4. Consent and updates

On your first visit to the site a banner is shown that lets you accept or reject cookies and tracking technologies used for analytics and marketing. The GoHighLevel attribution tracking script is loaded only if you give your consent; if you reject, it is not activated. Your choice is stored on your device and you can change it at any time by clearing your browser's browsing data, so that the banner reappears.

Should further analytics or marketing tools be activated (for example Meta Pixel or Google Analytics), this page will be updated accordingly.

For information on the processing of personal data see the Privacy Policy.
