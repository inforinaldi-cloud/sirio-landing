This notice describes how the personal data of users who browse this site and complete the contact form is processed, pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”).

For any request regarding your data you can write to: info@sergiorinaldiconsulting.com.

2. What data we collect

We only process the data you voluntarily provide through the strategic-analysis request form:

  • First and last name

This data is used solely to ensure the correct functioning of the site and for security purposes.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Responding to your request for a strategic analysis and contacting you again for related commercial purposes: legal basis is your consent and the performance of pre-contractual measures requested by the data subject (art. 6.1 lett. a and b GDPR).
  • Security and technical operation of the site: legal basis is the controller's legitimate interest (art. 6.1 lett. f GDPR).

Providing the data marked as mandatory in the form is necessary in order to follow up on your request; failure to provide it makes it impossible to contact you.

4. Processing methods and tools

Data is processed with IT tools and with adequate security measures. The submission of the contact form is handled by the FormSubmit service, which receives the completed data and delivers it by email to the controller. This data may also flow into the CRM and marketing-automation platform GoHighLevel, used to manage contacts. These providers act as data processors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).

5. Data disclosure and extra-EU transfer

Your data is neither disseminated nor sold to third parties for their own marketing purposes. It may be processed by technical service providers (CRM, hosting, email) appointed as data processors. Some of these providers are based in the United States: in such cases the transfer takes place on the basis of the adequate safeguards provided for by the GDPR (for example the European Commission's standard contractual clauses).

6. Retention period

Data is retained for as long as necessary to manage your request and any commercial relationship that may result from it, and in any case no longer than the terms provided for by applicable law. You can request its deletion at any time.

7. Your rights

As a data subject you have the right to obtain access to your data, its rectification or deletion, restriction of processing, portability, as well as to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time (arts. 15-22 GDPR). To exercise these rights write to info@sergiorinaldiconsulting.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).
