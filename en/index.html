FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Sirio Agency only work with window companies?

Sirio Agency specialises in the construction sector and mainly works with manufacturers, resellers and installers of windows, pergolas, panoramic glazing, photovoltaics, renovations and other high-value products for the home.

Do you only handle advertising campaigns?

No. Campaigns are only one part of the system. We can also work on CRM, lead contact, qualification, appointment setting, automation, sales training, quotes and follow-up.

Are the appointments qualified?

Qualification is designed based on the requirements agreed with the company, such as geographical area, type of work, interest, property ownership and availability for a site visit or consultation.

Do you work throughout Italy?

Yes. Marketing, automation, qualification, consulting and training activities can be managed remotely. The strategy is adapted to the areas the company actually serves.

Can we use our own CRM?

Yes. We can consider integrating with the CRM you already use or set up a shared structure to monitor leads, appointments, quotes and sales.

How much advertising budget is needed?

The budget depends on product, territory, competition, objectives and sales capacity. During the strategic analysis we define a sustainable threshold consistent with the company's economic potential.

How long does it take to see the first results?

The first data can arrive after the campaigns are launched, but the assessment must consider the whole journey: lead, contact, appointment, quote and sale. Timing also depends on the company's sales cycle.

Can you work on our old database?

Yes. We can design reactivation activities through calls, automation and artificial intelligence, in compliance with the conditions applicable to the database and the available consents.

Do you also train our salespeople?

Yes. We can work on scripts, discovery, site visits, objection handling, quote presentation, follow-up and deal analysis.

How do we know if we're a good fit to work together?

The first step is a strategic analysis. We assess offer, territory, structure, margins, appointment-handling capacity and objectives. If we don't see realistic conditions to generate a return, we tell you transparently.
